# Privacy Policy — Optiflow

**Effective date:** 2026-11-23

---

## Data we collect

The app does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. The only
personal data we receive is what you choose to send through the website contact
form (see below).
We do not require account registration or login of any kind.

## Location

If you enable the GPS option for solar panel location, your device coordinates are used
**only** to request a solar irradiance forecast from a third-party weather service.
The coordinates are transmitted solely for that forecast request and are
**not** stored by us, logged, or used for any other purpose.

You can also enter your location manually — in that case GPS is never accessed.

## Electricity prices

The app fetches publicly available electricity price data from PSE (Polskie Sieci
Elektroenergetyczne) and ENTSO-E. No personal information is transmitted in these requests.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains **no** analytics SDKs, crash-reporting libraries, advertising networks,
or any other form of tracking. We do not know who uses the app or how.

## Website contact form

The contact form on the Optiflow website asks for your name, email address,
optionally your company, a topic and your message. We use these details only to
reply to you. The form is delivered by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which forwards
the message to our inbox and may keep a copy for a limited time according to
its own terms and data processing agreement. We keep the email only as long as the conversation needs
it, and you can ask us at any time to delete it.

The data controller is Piotr Ciszek (sole proprietorship registered in
Poland), the developer of Optiflow. To ask about your data or to
have a message deleted, write to **contact@optiflow-energy.com**. Web3Forms acts
as our data processor; its own privacy policy and data processing agreement
(web3forms.com) describe where and how it processes data.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your message, or steps you ask
for before a possible agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and (b)). We keep a message
for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless an agreement requires
longer. Web3Forms may process data outside the European Economic Area under the
safeguards in its data processing agreement. You have the right to access,
correct and delete your data, to object, and to complain to the Polish data
protection authority (Prezes UODO).

przekazuje wiadomość do naszej skrzynki i może przechowywać jej kopię przez
ograniczony czas zgodnie ze swoim regulaminem i umową powierzenia danych.
Wiadomość przechowujemy tylko tak długo, jak wymaga tego rozmowa, i w każdej
chwili możesz poprosić o jej usunięcie.

Administratorem danych jest Piotr Ciszek (jednoosobowa działalność

usunięcie wiadomości: **contact@optiflow-energy.com**. Web3Forms przetwarza dane w
naszym imieniu (podmiot przetwarzający); szczegóły w jego polityce prywatności i
umowie powierzenia (web3forms.com).

Podstawa prawna: nasz prawnie uzasadniony interes w odpowiedzi na wiadomość lub

dostępu do danych, ich sprostowania i usunięcia, sprzeciwu oraz skargi do Prezesa
UODO.
